Train your inner awareness.

InteroHB is a simple practice app for interoception based on heartbeat awareness. It assists users to notice and count their heartbeat, and compare that felt sense with readings from Apple Watch or a compatible consumer-grade heart rate device.

Start with Sense for a quick check-in, use Flow when you want a longer guided session, and review insights when you want to see how your awareness changes over time.

The practice loop

How InteroHB works

InteroHB keeps the loop intentionally simple: sense first, reveal second, reflect after.

01
Sense

Check in quickly

Notice, count what you feel, and then compare your estimate with the reading from your Apple Watch or connected consumer heart rate device.

02
Flow

Slow the pace

Use a longer, quieter session to spend more time noticing subtle changes in your internal state.

03
Insights

Review the pattern

Look back across sessions to see trends in estimates, confidence, and practice context.

Heart rate sources

Use Apple Watch or a Bluetooth heart rate device

Pick the heart rate source that fits your routine. Both work with Sense, Flow, and Insights.

New

Apple Watch

The InteroHB companion app for Apple Watch provides heart rate readings during your sessions, so you can practice without extra hardware.

  • Companion watch app: heart rate readings from your wrist during sessions
  • Apple Health: works with Apple Health, with your permission

Bluetooth LE heart rate device

Connect a compatible consumer-grade heart rate device that broadcasts over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), such as a chest strap or armband.

  • Standard BLE: uses the Bluetooth Heart Rate Service
  • Your choice: use whichever source you prefer
Why InteroHB

Built for everyday practice

InteroHB is designed for short, repeatable sessions that fit into normal days. The app keeps the focus on noticing, comparing, and learning from your own patterns.

  • Quick sessions: use Sense when you want a brief check-in
  • Longer sessions: use Flow when you want more time with the signal
  • Private history: review your own session trends on your device
  • Simple context: add notes that help explain what was happening around a session
